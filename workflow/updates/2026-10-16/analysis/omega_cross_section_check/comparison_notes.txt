Definition and conversion
physics_omega.f defines sigcm=d^2sigma/(dt dphi), microbarn/MeV^2/radian.
The active sig_LT_extrapolation sets LT=TT=0, so it is phi independent.
Hence d sigma/dt [microbarn/GeV^2] = 2*pi*10^6*sigcm in this specific model.
The stored branch t already means -t [GeV^2]. No abs(sigcm) is applied.

Model reproduction
For nominal Q2>2, set z=Q2, v=abs(u), D=(W^2-mp^2)^2, mp=0.93827231 GeV.
sigma_T=(20.16527-4.2124*v)/(z^2*D)
sigma_L=(12.2546-29.8629*v)/(z^4*D)
d sigma/dt=sigma_T+epsilon*sigma_L.
This reproduces stored sigcm after unit conversion to maximum relative
difference 6.11e-7. All 595 longitudinal components are negative; 231 transverse
components are negative. The active backward-angle extrapolation is therefore
not a physical forward-region cross section for this sample.

Positive weights do not cure this
physics_omega.f:234,249,264 multiplies sigcm by a signed fixed-mass Jacobian
and virtual-photon flux. siglab/sigcm is negative for every accepted event.
event.f:1753-1779 subsequently includes Coulomb focusing and generation factors.
The positive event weights are compatible with negative sigcm and a negative
conversion factor. They do not independently validate the photon cross section.
The earlier 101.582 events/h arithmetic is reproducible, but should not be
presented as a validated physical rate until the model and Jacobian are resolved.
