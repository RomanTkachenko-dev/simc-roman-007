# Historical reports: read the current caveats first

These documents preserve the earlier calculation and meeting materials. They were prepared before the later omega-model diagnosis and have not been rewritten to imply a corrected physical result.

**The estimate of approximately 102 omega events/hour at 70 microampere is not validated:** all 595 accepted baseline omega events have negative model `sigcm`, even though their stored laboratory weights are positive. The arithmetic is preserved for audit, not endorsed as an absolute yield prediction.
